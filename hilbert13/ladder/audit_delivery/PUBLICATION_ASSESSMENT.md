## Assessment

There is a credible, substantial specialist-paper core, provided the algebraic chain receives independent review and the accessory definition is aligned precisely with the literature. The sharp values $a(A_7)=60$ and $\mu(A_7)=90$ are the strongest group-specific results. They concern all faithful curves and the generic torsor rather than a numerical model. The sharp genus theorem for three pencils is a second potentially broad contribution.

The spectral work is a serious computer-assisted application. Its representation-theoretic mechanism is promising, but its sparse arithmetic trust base remains explicit. Projective equations and syzygies provide discoveries; several stronger assertions need exact rank, root-count and scheme certificates.

The work addresses a specific accessory problem related to Hilbert 13. It does not determine the general septic's resolvent degree or prove $\mathrm{RD}(A_7)>1$.

## What the checked literature establishes

Farb–Wolfson's 2025 **preprint** proves the $A_7$ accessory obstruction through degree 6. Under the actual generic-torsor convention, the present theorem gives sharp threshold 60: degree at most 59 cannot compress to a curve, and degree 60 can. Their text recalls Klein's $L_2(7)$ upper bound 4 and leaves degree 3 open. The claimed value 4 would complete that comparison.

This accessory theorem is algebraic and independent of FEM, harmonic Hersch and the numerical $\mathbb P^5$ model. The disconnected-target definition must require actual torsor pullback; a faithful permutation of rational components describes a different problem.

Hassett–Tschinkel, Duncan–Sharma and Scavia–Tschinkel–Zhang supply an existing Amitsur framework. The candidate contribution is the compression-degree identity with the Albanese hypothesis and its explicit $A_7$ application. Laza–Zheng, Yang–Yu–Zhu and Koike establish the exceptional cubic beforehand; constructing these curves on it is the candidate contribution.

Multiple-pencil genus bounds and explicit syzygy constructions have substantial precedents. Guaranteed eigenvalue bounds and hyperbolic spectral algorithms also predate the project. The search supports no first-certified-spectrum, genus-record, or first-guaranteed-FEM claim.

No matching prior statement of the sharp values, global lower gonality 25, or the exact three-pencil hypotheses and constant was located in the checked sources. This is evidence for contribution candidates, not an exhaustive priority theorem. MathSciNet/zbMATH were not exhaustively searched.

## Submission standard

For an algebraic paper, define compression as actual torsor pullback, including induced targets. Give the universal incidence upper bound on any faithful linear model. Supply subgroup classification, signature termination, exact 84-case inputs and hashes. Isolate compound, adjugate and Halphen lemmas. Arrange independent review of the new genus theorem and all small accessory values.

For a spectral paper, export an independently checkable certificate. Either verify sparse-factor residuals outwardly or justify the precise CHOLMOD operation model. Record shifts, precision, cache provenance and failed runs. Lean abstracts are not a formalization of the full result.

For projective geometry, replace sampled ranks with exact minors/modular witnesses, certify roots/multiplicities, and establish ideal scheme structure. Proposition 7.7 additionally needs off-fixed-point orbit exclusion; immersion alone does not suffice.

The supported assessment is **a substantial sharp algebraic core, a potentially general geometric theorem, and a credible computer-assisted gonality program with delimited certification work**. More than one focused paper is plausible. Guaranteed acceptance, revolutionary impact or a solution to Hilbert 13 is not supported.
