# The full-monodromy compression degree of $A_7$ is 90

**Theorem.** $\mu(A_7)=90$.
- Here $\mu(G)$ is the least degree of a $G$-linearised, base-point-free line bundle with $h^0\ge2$ on a faithful connected
  $G$-curve.
- Equivalently (DAY 2, Thm 1.2 and Prop. 1.4): the least accessory degree for a curve compression of the generic $A_7$-torsor
  that keeps connected full $A_7$-monodromy is exactly 90.
- Compare $a(A_7)=60$, where the monodromy is allowed to drop to $L_2(7)$ (`ACCESSORY_60.md`).

**Inputs.**
- $\mu\ge72$ and the candidate list $\{72,78,84,90,\dots\}$: GPT Round 5, Thm 2.2 and Prop. 2.4; verified in `ROUND5_REVIEW.md`.
- $\mu\le90$: $h^0(B+T)\ge10$ on every $(2,4,7)$ curve (`equivariant_rr.py`, `ROUND5_REVIEW.md` §3).

**New here.** The exclusion of 72 and 84; 78 has no lattice-compatible signature. Every finite input is rechecked by
`verify_mu90.py` (output: `mu90_output.txt`, 168 curve/class/orientation cases, all closed).

## 1. Two frameworks

**(A) Orbit-semigroup base locus.**
- Setup: $(C,L)$ with $L$ linearised of degree $n$, $W\subset H^0(L)$ a $G$-subrepresentation, $f\in\mathrm{Sym}^kW$ a $G$-invariant.
- $f|_C$ is an invariant section of $L^k$, so its divisor is a sum of $G$-orbits.
- Hence **if $kn$ is not a nonnegative combination of orbit sizes, $f$ vanishes on $\varphi_W(C)$.**
- Consequence: the image curve lies in an explicit invariant variety. For $W=6$ these are power-sum varieties; for $W=10$ it
  is the determinantal quartic.

**(B) Spin geometry of the 10.** $A_7\subset SO(6)$ lifts to $2.A_7\subset\mathrm{Spin}(6)=SU(4)$. On the half-spin
representation $V_4$, $\wedge^2V_4=6$ and $\mathrm{Sym}^2V_4=\overline{10}$ (checked). So a curve with section representation 10
is a family of symmetric $4\times4$ matrices $Q_p$, and the rank of $Q_p$ is an invariant stratification.

**Parity lemma.** Let $c$ fix $p\in C$, and let $s$ be a lift of $c$ to $2.A_7$. Then $Q_p$ is an eigenvector of $c$ with eigenvalue
$\lambda=a^{-k}$, where $a$ is the rotation at $p$ and $k$ the multiplicity of the divisor at that fibre. If $\lambda$ is not $\mu^2$
for any eigenvalue $\mu$ of $s$ on $V_4$, then every element of that eigenspace has **even rank**.

*Proof.* In an eigenbasis of $s$, $Q_{ab}\ne0$ only if $\mu_a\mu_b=\lambda$. The map $\mu\mapsto\lambda/\mu$ is a fixed-point-free involution on
the eigenvalues, so the matrix is a direct sum of blocks $\begin{pmatrix}0&M\\M^T&0\end{pmatrix}$. $\blacksquare$

## 2. Proof that $\mu\ne72,84$

Let $\mu=n$ and let $L$ realise it on $C$. Minimality makes every linearised base-point-free subsystem birational onto its
image. So $g(C)\le\pi(n,h^0-1)$, and the signature has $N(C)\mid n$. With the seven-sheeted test and the existence
of generating triples, the candidates are:

| $n$ | signatures (genus) | $h^0\le$ |
|---|---|---|
| 72 | $(2,5,7)$ (199) | 12 |
| 72 | $(3,5,7)$, $(4,5,7)$, $(5,5,7)$ | 7, 6, 6 |
| 84 | $(3,4,5)$ (274), $(3,5,6)$ (379) | 12, 10 |
| 84 | $(4,5,6)$, $(5,5,6)$, $(5,6,6)$, $(5,6,7)$; $(2,2,3,5)$, $(2,2,5,6)$, $(2,3,3,5)$ | $\le8$ |

$n=78$ has no candidate. Every orbit has size $>n$, so $W=H^0(L)$ has no trivial summand. Hence $W\in\{6,10,\overline{10},6\oplus6\}$.

**Case $W\supseteq6$** (framework A). Let $x_1,\dots,x_7$ be the coordinates of the standard 6, so $\sum x_j=0$ and the power sums $p_k$ are invariant.
- **$n=72$.** $p_2,p_3,p_4,p_6$ vanish ($144,216,288,432$ are not sums of orbit sizes). So $e_1=e_2=e_3=e_4=e_6=0$, and the
  image lies in $X=\{x:\ x_j \text{ are the roots of } t^7-ut^2-v\}$.
  - $X$ is irreducible: the monodromy of $t^7-t^2$ is $S_7$ (six simple critical points and a 7-cycle).
  - $X$ is generically reduced (Jacobian rank 5 at points with distinct coordinates) and has degree $1\cdot2\cdot3\cdot4\cdot6=144$.
  - So the image, of degree $\le72$, cannot be a component. Contradiction.
- **$n=84$.** $p_2,p_3,p_4,p_7$ vanish. Newton's identities then give $p_7=7e_7$, so $\prod x_j=0$ on $C$. The image would lie in one
  coordinate hyperplane, hence by transitivity in all. Contradiction.

**Case $W\in\{10,\overline{10}\}$** (only on $(2,5,7)$ at 72, and $(3,4,5)$, $(3,5,6)$ at 84). Framework A gives $\det Q_p\equiv0$, since
$4n<\min$ orbit. So the generic rank $r$ is 1, 2 or 3.
- **$r=2$.** $\wedge^2Q_p=\pm(v\wedge w)^{\otimes2}$ gives an equivariant map to $\mathbb P(\wedge^2V_4)=\mathbb P(6)$. Its bundle $N$ is linearised and
  base-point-free, with $N^2=L^2(-B')$. Then $\mu\le\deg N\le n=\mu$, so $\deg N=n$ and $N$ is a minimal bundle containing a 6. This is
  the previous case.
- **$r=3$.** The adjugate gives the kernel map $\kappa$ with $\kappa^*\mathcal O(2)=L^3(-B'')$, so $\deg B''\le3n$. In every case some branch eigenspace
  contains no rank-3 matrix with $\det=0$ (the $e=2$, 4 or 6 branch respectively). There $\mathrm{rank}\,Q_p\le2$, so $B''$ contains that orbit,
  whose size is $1260$, $630$ or $420$, all $>3n$. Contradiction.
- **$r=1$.** $Q_p=v_p\otimes v_p$ everywhere.
  - $(2,5,7)$, $(3,4,5)$: the parity lemma applies at the $e=2$, resp. $e=4$, branch. The rank there is even, not 1.
  - $(3,5,6)$: two of its three classes have no parity branch. There $\varphi=v_2\circ\psi$ with $\psi:C\to\mathbb P^3$ of degree $42$.
    - $\psi$ is birational: otherwise the image has degree $\le21$ and genus $\le\pi(21,3)=90<136$.
    - $\psi(C)$ lies on no quadric, because $\mathrm{Sym}^2V_4$ is irreducible.
    - Halphen's bound (Gruson–Peskine) then gives $g\le d(d-3)/6+1=274<379$.

So $\mu\notin\{72,78,84\}$, and $\mu=90$. $\blacksquare$
