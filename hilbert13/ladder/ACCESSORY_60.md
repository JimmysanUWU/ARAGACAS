# The accessory threshold of $A_7$ is 60

**Source.** GPT (Astra), *A7 curves and accessory degrees — a consolidated mathematical reference*, 1 October 2026
("DAY 2"), §§1–3. The theorem and its proof are GPT's. This file records our independent verification (sixth session)
and its consequences for the ladder. Exact checks are in `verify_accessory60.py`.

## 1. Statement

The convention: an accessory is an arbitrary finite extension $F/K$ of $K=\mathbb C(V)^{A_7}$. After the accessory, the
Galois group of the torsor may drop to a subgroup $H$. A *curve compression* is a faithful $H$-stable curve field
$E\subset LF$ with $LF=FE$. Let $a(G)$ be the least accessory degree allowing curve compression. Then
$$a(A_7)=60,\qquad\text{i.e.}\qquad \mathrm{ed}_{\mathbb C}(A_7;\le59)>1,\quad \mathrm{ed}_{\mathbb C}(A_7;\le60)=1 .$$
The lower bound also holds when compressions must keep connected full-$A_7$ monodromy, since $a\le\mu(A_7)$ (§2).

## 2. The mechanism

$\mu(G)$ is the least degree of a $G$-**linearised**, base-point-free line bundle with $h^0\ge2$ on a faithful connected
$G$-curve. The proof has four steps.

1. **Correspondence (Thm 1.2).** Take an equivariant degree-$d$ cover $W\dashrightarrow V$ and a dominant equivariant map
   $W\dashrightarrow C$.
   - The closure $Z$ of the joint image in $V\times C$ is an invariant Cartier divisor, so $\mathcal O(Z)$ is canonically linearised.
   - Restrict it to $\{0\}\times C$; here $0\in V$ is fixed.
   - $\mathrm{Pic}(V\times C)=\mathrm{Pic}(C)$, so every fibre has the same class: an effective divisor of degree $e\mid d$ that moves.
   - Removing the invariant fixed part gives $\mu(G)\le d$.
2. **Subgroup induction (Thm 1.5).** $a(G)=\min_{H\le G}[G:H]\,\mu(H)$. For the lower bound, take $H$ = monodromy after the
   accessory, $[F:K]=[G:H]e$, and apply step 1 to $H$. The upper bound uses induction from $H$ plus the incidence
   variety of Prop. 1.4.
3. **Degree lattice (Thm 2.1).** A linearised bundle has degree in $N_G(C)\mathbb Z$, with $N_G(C)=|G|/\mathrm{lcm}(e_i)$. This is our
   Lemma 7.1 in `FRAMEWORK_TOPOLOGICAL_HERSCH.md`, proved the same way: Hilbert 90 gives an invariant rational section.
4. **Linearised Castelnuovo (Prop. 2.2).** Let $m<|G|$ with $G$ simple. Map by the complete series. The kernel on the image
   has order at most $m$, so it is trivial, and the image $Y$ is a faithful $G$-curve. The sections form a genuine,
   nontrivial representation, so $r\ge q(G)-1$, which is 5 for $A_7$. Pulling back $\mathcal O(1)$ gives a linearised
   bundle on $Y$, so $N_G(Y)\mid n\le m$ and $g(Y)\le\pi(n,r)\le\pi(n,q(G)-1)$.

**Theorem 3.1: $\mu(A_7)\ge60$.**
- All inertia orders divide 420, so $6\mid N$ and $n\le54$.
- Then $g(Y)\le\pi(54,5)=325$.
- In genus 136–325 the only lattice-compatible cases are $(2,5,7)$ with $n=36$ ($\pi=136<199$) and $(3,4,5)$ with $n=42$
  ($\pi=190<274$). Both are contradictions.

**Subgroups of index below 60.**

| $H$ | index | $\mu(H)$ lower bound | product |
|---|---|---|---|
| $A_6$ | 7 | 9 | 63 |
| $L_2(7)$ | 15 | 4 | 60 |
| $S_5$ | 21 | 3 | 63 |
| $(A_4\times C_3){:}2$ | 35 | 2 | 70 |
| $A_5$ | 42 | 2 | 84 |

Why these bounds hold:
- $A_6$: $\pi(8,4)=5<10$, the minimum genus of $A_6$.
- $L_2(7)$: $\pi(3,2)=1<3$.
- $S_5$: a hyperelliptic involution is central, so $S_5$ would embed in $\mathrm{PGL}_2$, which is impossible.
- $(A_4\times C_3){:}2$: it contains $C_3^2$, which does not embed in $\mathrm{PGL}_2$.
- $A_5$: it has no 2-dimensional linear representation.

**Upper bound.** $L_2(7)$ (index 15) with the canonical bundle of the Klein quartic: $15\cdot4=60$.

**Sanity check.** The same formula gives $a(A_5)=2$, which is Kronecker–Klein's square-root accessory irrationality
for the quintic.

## 4. Consequences for this repository

- **The ed question is settled at 60.** It needs no gonality or spectral input. The earlier reductions
  ("$\mathrm{ed}(A_7;\le23)>1$ from $\operatorname{gon}\ge19$", "$\mathrm{ed}(A_7;\le29)>1$ from $\operatorname{gon}\ge25$") are true but superseded.
- **Gonality is now a separate problem: what is $\gamma(A_7)$, and is $\operatorname{gon}\ge25$ true for all faithful $A_7$-curves?** The

  - Spectral bounds beat the algebraic table (DAY 2, p. 11) in every row.
